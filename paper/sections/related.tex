\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Spectra of trained weights and fine-tuning.}
Trained-weight spectra exhibit outliers and ``bulk plus spikes'' regimes
\citep{martin2021}; small singular directions can also carry fine-tuning edits
\citep{staats2024small}. LoRA introduces high-ranking ``intruder dimensions''
absent under matched full fine-tuning \citep{shuttleworth2025lora}. These works
characterize trained weights or adaptation schemes. We instead analyze the
checkpoint increment $\Delta W$ and intervene on its leading directions.

\paragraph{Low-rank adaptation and weight arithmetic.}
Low intrinsic dimension and LoRA show that adaptation can be
parameter-efficient \citep{aghajanyan2021,hu2022lora}; task arithmetic edits
behavior with whole checkpoint deltas
\citep{ilharco2023task,ortizjimenez2023task}. Contrastive weight steering
subtracts opposed fine-tuning updates to obtain a behavior direction
\citep{fierro2026weight}. Our medical contrast uses that construction, then adds
a fitted spectral census, subspace localization, matched-fold comparisons, and
residual-stream interventions. We do not claim priority for contrastive weight
arithmetic.

\paragraph{Safety-relevant parameter subspaces.}
Safety-critical neuron and rank regions can be identified through pruning and
low-rank modification \citep{wei2024brittleness}. Safe LoRA, LSSF, and
safety-preserving fine-tuning use low-rank alignment subspaces to preserve or
restore safety \citep{hsu2024safelora,zhou2025lssf,zhang2026preserving}. Our
global residual projection instead diagnoses behavioral sensitivity and causes
broad capability loss.

\paragraph{Activation directions and interpretability.}
RepE reads and steers concept directions \citep{zou2023repe}, while sparse
autoencoders surface steerable features \citep{templeton2024scaling}.
\citet{arditi2024} recover refusal by an activation difference of means. We ask
whether a leading weight-increment subspace is enriched for that independently
estimated activation direction. Figure~\ref{fig:geometry} is construction-only;
the projection audit supplies the behavioral test.

\paragraph{Emergent misalignment and behavioral auditing.}
Narrow fine-tuning can induce broad misalignment \citep{betley2025emergent},
with convergent activation directions \citep{soligo2025convergent} and even a
single rank-one LoRA adapter \citep{turner2025organisms}. Supervised deception
probes and hidden-objective audits use richer behavioral evidence
\citep{goldowskydill2025,marks2025}. Our checkpoint screens use arm labels but
no judged responses; they are not competitors to behavior-labeled probes, and
their interventions remain in-sample.

\paragraph{Adjacent spectral diagnostics.}
Spectral backdoor detectors score per-input activations \citep{tran2018spectral};
LARF compares effective rank of harmful-prompt representation transformations
across fine-tuning subsets \citep{larf2025}. Those observables differ from a
checkpoint increment. Our fitted MP reference prioritizes directions for tests;
it is neither a calibrated detector nor an optimization model.

\paragraph{Complementary mechanisms.}
Catastrophic forgetting, representational reorganization, and gradient
interference can all shape endpoint deltas
\citep{kirkpatrick2017,merchant2020,yu2020gradient}. Spectral geometry describes
the resulting anisotropy and nominates subspaces; it does not identify which
process produced them.
